## Long-term vision

_(Note: This is a broader underlying purpose guiding our iterative refinement process—not an immediate, single-step objective. It provides context and direction, guiding the iterative refinement of practical steps.)_

My long-term vision is to establish an explicit prompting strategy that consistently induces AI assistants (ChatGPT or selected AI models) to demonstrate practical **capability-awareness** in follow-up responses. Specifically, the AI assistant should explicitly:

- **Articulate its own capabilities and limitations** clearly and transparently.
- **Recognize when external AI models or tools** are better suited or complementary, clearly indicating why and how they exceed or complement its internal capabilities.
- **Proactively suggest external tool usage** by providing clear, concise, actionable prompts I can directly copy and invoke.
- **Support iterative refinement** by explicitly indicating its confidence levels, areas of uncertainty, and recommending Mediator edits whenever necessary for improved outcomes.

This strategy does **not** aim for subjective, human-like self-awareness. Instead, it emphasizes practical, explicitly-engineered "AI capability-awareness" as outlined in the deep research analysis. It leverages proven prompting techniques (direct capability-assessment prompts, confidence queries, structured introspective reasoning, and explicit self-checks) to simulate effective introspection within the realistic constraints of current AI systems.

Ultimately, I aim to create a stable, clear, and practical workflow enabling AI assistants to continuously:

1. Demonstrate explicit awareness of their built-in capabilities, knowledge boundaries, and operational limitations.
2. Clearly communicate when external AI tools or models offer superior or complementary capability.
3. Suggest iterative improvements through explicit feedback, checkpoints, and editing recommendations, achieving a robust, predictable, and practical capability-awareness behavior through careful prompt engineering.
